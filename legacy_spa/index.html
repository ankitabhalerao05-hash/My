About PathFinder AI

Learn more about our mission, AI technologies, and our vision for career guidance.

Our Mission

PathFinder is dedicated to helping students and aspiring professionals navigate their career journeys. By leveraging advanced artificial intelligence, we analyze complex profiles and map them to standard industry roles, helping you make informed career choices with confidence.

Our platform combines cutting-edge predictive modeling with actual learning curriculums, books, and industry benchmarks, bridging the gap between student aspirations and career readiness.

AI-Driven Mentorship

Personalized roadmap milestones, automated ATS keyword checks, and conversational career counseling with Aura AI Coach.

Core Platform Pillars

Career Guidance

Machine learning evaluations matching academic profiles directly to fitting job domains.

Resume Analytics

Automated parser calculating keyword ATS alignment and tracking profile highlights.

Skill Assessments

Gamified Skill Challenges awarding XP points and unlocking achievement badges.

Milestone Roadmaps

Horizontal flows outlining phase actions, exam goals, and portfolio projects.

Our Vision

We envision a world where career guidance is democratized and easily accessible to all candidates regardless of their location. By utilizing localized predictive models and Gemini-powered analytics, we aim to equip every student with their own personalized 24/7 AI mentor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data saved securely?

Yes, all data, resume parser text, credentials, and achievements are saved locally in your browser's private database storage.

How does the AI model evaluate profiles?

If you supply a Gemini API Key, it runs real-time LLM predictions. Otherwise, it uses local statistical prediction fallbacks.

How do I earn XP points?

Complete the Skill Challenge Quiz! You get +100 XP for every correct response.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic credentials and profile statistics provided by you during registration and profile building, including Name, Email address, Degree paths, Majors, Skill sets, and local database attachments.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to generate career path recommendations, analyze resume formatting keyword matches, track upskilling history milestones, and update leaderboard metrics.

3. AI Processing Disclaimer & Resume Uploads

Resume uploads and AI conversations are processed specifically to provide career guidance and improve your user experience. If you supply a Google Gemini API key, profile packets are safely sent to Google's LLM endpoints for analysis. We do not store or process these files on external servers.

4. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We use local storage cookies to handle active login tokens. Google SSO integration uses secure Google Authentication channels to verify client identities without extracting passwords.

5. Data Security & Retention

All portfolio items and credentials are saved locally in browser IndexedDB formats. Data is retained until you delete your account or clear local cache storage.

6. User Rights & Contact Info

You have full rights to clear, export, or delete your profile data inside the profile settings panel at any time. For questions, contact us at support@pathfinder.ai.
